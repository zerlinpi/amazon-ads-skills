## Minimal read-only fix
For requests with a concrete `feature_eligibility_requirement`, compare the connector snapshot's `captured_at` with `feature_eligibility_requirement.as_of` as known-offset UTC instants. If the snapshot postdates as_of, or its capture instant is missing, invalid or uses an unknown RFC3339 offset, expose `feature_snapshot_chronology_effect=unknown` and degrade the overall gate (unless another independent condition blocks). Equal/earlier captures pass this **chronology** subgate only. Calls without a feature requirement yield `not_evaluated`. Verified Ineligible still blocks. No global connector TTL is invented: explicit connector `freshness_requirement` and independently timestamped binding evidence retain their own checks. The feature eligibility horizon is **not** silently applied as connector freshness. This normalized source-time consistency test cannot authenticate raw HTTP, prove enrollment, verify historical connector availability, or authorize writes.
